About Phadak

A focused trading desk with a direct line to the decision-maker

How we work

A clear, bankable process

  1. Enquiry

    Send product, volume, destination and your LOI or ICPO.

  2. Offer

    We issue a Full Corporate Offer with price basis, specification and schedule.

  3. Contract

    Both sides sign the SPA. The buyer's bank issues an irrevocable documentary L/C.

  4. Loading

    Independent inspection confirms quality and quantity as the cargo loads.

  5. Settlement

    Shipping documents are presented to the bank and payment is released.

Compliance

Due diligence on every deal

Physical commodity trading attracts fraud. We protect our counterparties and ourselves with the same checks on every transaction.

  • KYC on every counterparty. Company registration, ownership and signatory checks before any offer.
  • Sanctions screening. Parties, vessels and origin are checked against UN, EU, UK and US lists.
  • Verified title. We confirm the seller holds or controls the product before we offer it.
  • Bank-to-bank payment. Payment by documentary L/C through recognised banks only.
  • Independent inspection. A third-party surveyor confirms every cargo.

We never ask for upfront fees

Phadak does not charge buyers fees before shipment, and we do not ask for proof of funds before we have verified the product. If anyone asks you for advance payment in our name, contact us directly at the details below.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the product. Sulphur and petrochemicals can start with a trial lot, and crude and refined products are usually a full or part cargo. Tell us your volume and we will say what is possible.

How is the price set?

Most cargoes are priced against Platts or Argus benchmarks plus or minus a premium, or at a fixed price for smaller lots. The basis is stated in our offer and the SPA.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Our standard is an irrevocable, confirmed documentary letter of credit (MT700) from a recognised bank. Other bank instruments can be discussed case by case after KYC.

Who carries out the inspection?

An independent surveyor such as SGS, Intertek or Bureau Veritas inspects at the load port. The buyer may appoint its own surveyor at its own cost.

What documents do I receive?

A standard set includes the bill of lading, commercial invoice, certificates of quality, quantity and origin, and, for CIF deals, the insurance certificate. Product-specific documents such as an assay or MSDS are added as needed.

What do you need from me to start?

Your company details for KYC, and the product, specification, quantity, delivery term and destination. An LOI or ICPO speeds things up.
